# Skyscanner Backpack — thin edition

A trimmed copy of [samples/backpack](../../samples/backpack/SOURCE.md), for quick checks on a small but realistic system. Made by `node scripts/thin.mjs`; not edited by hand.

- Kept: Core, text, surface, line and status colors; button colors; spacing, radii, font weights and type settings. The aliases they point at come along; the rest of the palette does not.
- Also kept: every token those point at, so no reference is broken by the trimming.
- Tokens are as published: same names, values, types and descriptions. Only whole tokens, files and themes were left out.
- 285 tokens in 1 files.
