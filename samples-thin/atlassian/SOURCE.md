# Atlassian Design System — thin edition

A trimmed copy of [samples/atlassian](../../samples/atlassian/SOURCE.md), for quick checks on a small but realistic system. Made by `node scripts/thin.mjs`; not edited by hand.

- Kept: Light and dark (not the increased-contrast themes); text, icon, border and link colors, the neutral, brand, status, input, selected and disabled backgrounds, and the surfaces. No accent colors.
- Also kept: every token those point at, so no reference is broken by the trimming.
- Tokens are as published: same names, values, types and descriptions. Only whole tokens, files and themes were left out.
- 298 tokens in 2 files.
