# Designsystemet (Norwegian Digitalisation Agency) — thin edition

A trimmed copy of [samples/designsystemet](../../samples/designsystemet/SOURCE.md), for quick checks on a small but realistic system. Made by `node scripts/thin.mjs`; not edited by hand.

- Kept: Light and dark, one size and one typeface; the accent, neutral, success and danger colors; the semantic style file.
- Also kept: every token those point at, so no reference is broken by the trimming.
- Tokens are as published: same names, values, types and descriptions. Only whole tokens, files and themes were left out.
- 461 tokens in 13 files.
